Trimming & Pruning Services

Professional Pruning for Healthier, Safer Trees

Every cut we make has a purpose. Whether you need hazard reduction, clearance pruning, or seasonal shaping, we approach every tree with care and precision.

  • Crown Thinning Selectively removing interior branches to improve light penetration, airflow, and reduce wind load — keeping trees healthy and storm-resistant.
  • Deadwood Removal Dead, dying, and structurally compromised branches are removed before they fall. Protects your family, roof, and vehicles below.
  • Canopy Raising Removing lower limbs to increase clearance over driveways, walkways, and structures. Improves sightlines and reduces ground-level hazards.
  • Vista Pruning Strategic branch removal to open up views while preserving the natural character and long-term health of your trees.
  • Storm-Damage Pruning After a storm, broken and hanging branches must be properly cut to prevent further tearing and disease entry. We clean up storm damage correctly.
  • Seasonal Maintenance Trimming Regular annual or bi-annual trimming keeps trees in peak shape, prevents overgrowth, and saves money by catching issues early.

When to Trim

Year-Round Trimming in San Antonio

San Antonio's climate means trees can be trimmed almost any time — but timing matters for optimal health and results.

Winter (Dec–Feb)

The best time for major structural pruning. Trees are dormant, wounds heal fast in spring, and pests are inactive. Ideal for oak trees to avoid oak wilt.

Spring (Mar–May)

Light shaping and dead wood removal after winter. Avoid cutting live oaks during high oak wilt season (Feb–Jun). Great time for canopy raising.

Summer (Jun–Aug)

Hazard trimming and storm-prep work. We address dead limbs and overhang before peak storm season hits. Keeps your property protected during hurricanes and severe weather.

Fall (Sep–Nov)

Great for thinning dense canopies before winter storms. Removing weight from heavy branches reduces wind-load risk when cold fronts bring strong gusts to Bexar County.
