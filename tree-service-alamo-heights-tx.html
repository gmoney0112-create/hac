Seasonal Guide

Keeping Mature Trees Healthy

Mature trees reward good timing. General guidelines below. Ask us about your specific trees.

Winter

Best window for major pruning of live oaks and structural work.

Spring

Avoid pruning healthy live oaks from roughly February through June. Focus on deadwood, hazards, and non-oak trees.

Summer

Remove dead limbs and overhang ahead of storm season.

Fall

Good for planned thinning and canopy raising once the risky season is over.
