What to Expect

Before We Arrive

We confirm the stump size and location via free estimate. You'll know exactly what to expect — the grind depth, the backfill method, and the final cost. No surprise charges on job day.

During the Grind

Our stump grinder reduces the stump and major surface roots to wood chip mulch. The process is loud but fast — most single stumps are done in under an hour. We protect nearby plants, edging, and irrigation lines throughout.

After Completion

The void is backfilled and leveled with grindings. Excess chips are raked off your lawn. Within 4–6 weeks the grindings decompose enough to lay sod or topsoil over the area. We'll advise you on the best next step for your yard.

Note: Utility locates (call 811) are the homeowner's responsibility before grinding. We recommend locating utilities 3 business days before your appointment.

Why Remove Stumps

The Real Problems Stumps Cause

That old stump isn't just an eyesore. Left in place, stumps create a cascade of problems that cost far more to deal with later.

⚠️

Trip & Liability Hazard

Stumps hidden by grass are a leading cause of yard injuries. As a homeowner, you can be held liable if a guest trips on an unmarked stump on your property.

🪲

Pest Infestation Magnet

Decaying stumps attract termites, carpenter ants, wood-boring beetles, and other insects. Once established near your foundation, these pests are expensive to eradicate.

🌱

Unwanted Regrowth

Many tree species send up sucker shoots from old stumps. These new sprouts can push up through turf, pavers, and landscape fabric — requiring repeated removal.

🏡

Curb Appeal & Property Value

Stumps detract from your home's appearance and can negatively affect resale value. Buyers notice — and may request removal as a condition of sale.
