Emergency Situations We Handle

We Respond to Any Tree Emergency

When a tree becomes an immediate threat to life or property, you need someone who picks up the phone — not a voicemail. our team responds immediately, assesses the hazard, and gets to work.

  • Fallen Trees on Structures Tree on your roof, car, fence, or outbuilding. We assess the structural load, stabilize what's needed, and remove the tree safely to prevent further damage.
  • Storm-Damaged Limbs & Hangers "Widow makers" — broken limbs hanging in the canopy — are the most dangerous outcome of severe weather. We identify and remove every hazardous hanger before they fall.
  • Trees Blocking Roads or Driveways Fallen trees blocking access to your home or street require immediate response. We dispatch quickly and clear the obstruction to restore access and safety.
  • Post-Storm Property Assessment After major storms, hidden damage is everywhere. We walk your entire property and identify every damaged or destabilized tree before it becomes the next emergency.
  • Immediate Hazard Mitigation When a tree is leaning dangerously or at risk of imminent failure, we take immediate action to secure or remove it before it falls and causes injury or damage.
  • Insurance Documentation Support Filing a homeowner's insurance claim? We provide detailed documentation of damage, cause, and scope of work to support your claim and make the process easier.

Common Emergencies

Is Your Situation an Emergency?

If you're unsure whether to call, err on the side of calling. We will tell you honestly whether you need immediate response or can schedule for the next day.

🏠

Tree on Your Home

A tree resting on your roof or walls is a structural emergency. Water intrusion begins within hours. Call immediately — do not wait until morning.

⚡

Tree Near Power Lines

Never touch or approach a tree in contact with power lines. Call 911 and your utility company first, then call our team once lines are confirmed safe or de-energized.

🌪️

Post-Storm Damage

After a major storm, visible and invisible damage is present throughout the canopy. We assess the full property so nothing falls on your family or home days later.

🚗

Vehicle or Driveway Blocked

Tree blocking access to your home or vehicle. We clear it fast so you're not stranded — available same-night or first thing in the morning depending on scope.

😨

Leaning or Cracked Tree

A tree that has developed a sudden lean or visible trunk crack is at imminent risk of falling. This is an emergency — it needs assessment today, not next week.

🌧️

Storm Watch / Hurricane

Call before the storm hits. Pre-storm hazard trimming and removal is the best insurance you can get. We help San Antonio homeowners prepare before severe weather arrives.
